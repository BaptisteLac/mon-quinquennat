Fair and simple pricing

At Clever Cloud, our priority is to keep your applications running and your bills under control.

Thanks to auto-scalability, you choose the resources you need and you determine the maximum price you want to incur if your activity increases.

PRICE ESTIMATOR

How to choose your products?

Browse the list of all our products and services to create your estimation.

You can also choose a specific region.

Clever Cloud will automatically and continuously adapt the resources dedicated to your application, within the limits you have set.

All the resources deployed in this way are billed by the second, to reflect actual usage as closely as possible.

You’ll never pay more than you choose!

When you sign up you will get free credits to give Clever Cloud a try with no payment card required.

ENTERPRISE

Test now with free credits

If you are a business user and need more credits for your project, please contact us, we will be happy to answer your questions and provide more details on our startup and enterprise programs!

Better invoicing

Each month, you fund your Clever Cloud account with an amount that reflects your usual consumption, adjusted each month according to the actual consumption you have made.

And you receive an itemised bill explaining exactly what you have paid.
